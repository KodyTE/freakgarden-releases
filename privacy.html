Freak Garden Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

This policy explains what information the Freak Garden app for Android ("the game", "we", "us") handles, and why. Freak Garden is made by an independent developer, KodyTE.

The short version

  • Your garden is saved only on your device. We never receive it.
  • There are no accounts, and we don't collect your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device information to show and measure those ads, as described below.
  • You can turn off ad personalization, and in the EEA, UK and Switzerland you're asked for consent first.

1. Your save data stays on your device

The game stores your progress in the app's private storage on your phone or tablet. This includes your freaks, coins, items, garden layout, level, missions and settings. This data:

  • is never sent to us or to any server we run (we don't run any);
  • isn't linked to your identity, since the game has no sign-in;
  • is deleted when you use Reset save in the game's Settings, clear the app's data in Android settings, or uninstall the game.

Save codes. The Export save option turns your save into a text code on your device so you can move it to another copy of the game. The code is only shared if you choose to copy and paste it somewhere yourself.

Android may include app data in your device's own Google backup if you have backup turned on. That backup is handled by Google under your Google Account settings, not by us.

2. Information we don't collect

The game itself doesn't collect, store or send any personal information. It has no analytics or tracking of its own and doesn't access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or precise location. It never asks you to approve a permission. In the background it uses internet and network-status access for ads, vibration for button feedback (which runs entirely on your device), and the advertising ID and ad-privacy permissions that Google's ads software includes.

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

To keep the game free, it shows ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. Some ads are optional "rewarded" ads you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game bonus. When an ad is requested or shown, the AdMob software in the game may collect and send to Google:

  • your device's advertising ID (a resettable ID provided by Android) or a similar app-specific identifier;
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general (city-level) location;
  • device and app information such as device model, operating system version, language, screen size and app version;
  • ad interactions, such as which ads were shown, viewed or tapped;
  • diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data from the ads software.

Google uses this information to deliver ads, measure how they perform, prevent fraud and abuse and, where you allow it, show ads more relevant to you. Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's own policies:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob and data collection

We don't receive this information about you personally. AdMob only gives us combined reports, such as how many ads were shown and how much they earned.

4. Your choices about ads

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland). If you're in these regions, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalized ads are shown. You can review or change that choice at any time from the game's Settings.
  • Turn off personalized ads. On Android, go to Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads, depending on your device) to reset or delete your advertising ID. With no advertising ID, you'll still see ads, but they won't be personalized.
  • Rewarded ads are optional. You never have to watch one to keep playing.
  • US state privacy rights. Ad personalization can count as "sharing" or "targeted advertising" under some US state laws. You can opt out at any time using the device settings above.

5. Children

Freak Garden is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game in a way that caused information about them to be collected, contact us and we'll help you limit ad data collection for that device.

6. Security and retention

Your save data is kept in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps can't read, until you delete it as described above. We don't keep copies. Data collected by Google AdMob is sent over encrypted connections and kept according to Google's policies.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to certain processing. Because we hold no personal information about you, there's nothing for us to hand over or delete: your save lives only on your device. For data held by Google in connection with ads, you can use the device settings above or Google's privacy controls.

8. Changes to this policy

If the game's data practices change, for example if a new feature or service is added, we'll update this page and change the effective date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the game's release notes.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or your privacy? Contact the developer at freakmobileworldwide@gmail.com.
